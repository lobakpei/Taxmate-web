TaxMate UK privacy policy

Last updated 19 August 2026 · policy version 2026-08-19

Controller and contact

TaxMate UK (“TaxMate”) is a trading name used by Hau Ying Ou-Yang, a UK sole trader and the controller for this service. ICO registration: ZC174150. Email support@taxmate.uk. TaxMate has not appointed a data protection officer.

Information we process

TaxMate can process business names and types; income, expense, mileage, category, date and note records; tax-year adjustments; receipt images; backup files; partnership membership and shared records; and app preferences. Google sign-in supplies an account identifier and may supply your name, email and profile image. Billing information can include a Stripe customer identifier, subscription status, plan, renewal/cancellation status and promotion redemption. We also process support messages and limited technical diagnostics.

Do not enter special-category information or information about another person unless genuinely necessary for your records and you are entitled to use it.

Local and cloud operation

The app works locally without an account. Local records remain in your browser until you clear, reset or delete them. Google is the only sign-in provider. Sign-in is optional but required for cloud sync, partnership sync and paid plans. Signed-in personal records use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage. Partnership records are available to members of that partnership.

Providers and recipients

Google/Firebase processes sign-in, App Check, cloud records, receipt storage and server functions. Stripe processes checkout, payment methods, invoices, subscriptions and promotion codes; TaxMate does not receive complete card details. Sentry processes minimised error reports. With opt-in consent, Google Analytics 4 processes a small approved set of value-free events. Namecheap forwards messages sent to the public support address and Microsoft Outlook is the destination mailbox service; the private destination address is not published. Google Fonts and software-content delivery networks supply browser assets and receive ordinary connection metadata. Information may also be disclosed to professional advisers, regulators, courts or law-enforcement bodies where necessary and lawful.

TaxMate does not sell personal information or use bookkeeping records for advertising.

Locations and international transfers

The candidate Firestore database and server functions are configured for europe-west2 (London). Receipt Storage is configured in the United States. Google, Stripe, Sentry and their subprocessors may process information in the UK, EEA, United States and elsewhere. Provider terms describe applicable adequacy arrangements, the UK Extension to the Data Privacy Framework and/or contractual safeguards such as the UK Addendum to standard contractual clauses. Contact us for further information about safeguards.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it. Active-account cloud records remain while the account is used. The authenticated deletion workflow removes the personal Firestore tree, receipt objects, promotion-redemption records, TaxMate’s Stripe customer object and Firebase Auth identity. Where another partnership member remains, shared records remain for them and your membership is removed; the partnership is deleted when the last member leaves.

Provider backups, logs, fraud-prevention records and legally required transaction records may remain for limited provider-defined or legally required periods. GA4 user/event retention must be configured to two months before release; standard aggregated reports are not governed by that setting. Sentry event retention must be fixed and verified in staging before release. Deletion is complete only when the app reports server-side success.

Your choices and rights

You can use TaxMate locally, correct records, export JSON, export a full ZIP containing available receipt binaries, withdraw analytics consent in Settings and use “Delete all my data”. Depending on the basis and circumstances, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier processing.

Your right to object

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us. We will assess the request against compelling legitimate grounds and legal obligations.

Automated calculations and security

Rules-based tax estimates and plan entitlements do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. TaxMate is not affiliated with HMRC, does not submit returns or MTD updates, and is not tax advice. We use access rules, App Check, encrypted provider connections and telemetry filtering, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Complaints and changes

Email support@taxmate.uk about privacy or to exercise a right. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Material changes will be dated and communicated appropriately.
