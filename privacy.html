TaxMate UK privacy policy

Version 2026-08-17. TaxMate UK is operated by Hau Ying Ou-Yang in the United Kingdom. Contact support@taxmate.uk. ICO registration ZC174150.

TaxMate stores records on your device. Signed-in records use Google Firebase: Firestore data is in europe-west2 (London); receipt storage is in US-CENTRAL1. Stripe processes subscription and promotion status; TaxMate does not receive card details. Google Analytics 4 runs without client storage for aggregate usage. Sentry receives scrubbed error diagnostics without form values, financial records, receipt images, user identity or query strings.

You can export a JSON backup. Receipt binaries are listed but not embedded. Delete all data removes personal Firestore data, receipts, billing customer data and the Auth account through an authenticated server process. Shared partnership records remain available to other members; your membership is removed. Provider backup and Storage soft-delete retention may apply for limited recovery periods.

For access, correction, deletion or complaints, email support@taxmate.uk or contact the ICO.
