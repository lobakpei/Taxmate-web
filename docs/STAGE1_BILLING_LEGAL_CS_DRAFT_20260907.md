## Applicable facts and legal checks

The current product names Hau Ying Ou-Yang, trading as TaxMate UK, as a UK sole
trader. England/Wales law is stated, with mandatory protections elsewhere
preserved. The service targets UK bookkeeping; six UI languages do not establish
six separate sales jurisdictions. Record the actual purchaser, purchase purpose,
residence and contract before deciding a disputed case. A business ledger does
not itself prove that the purchaser was acting mainly in business. Consumer
status depends on the individual's actual purpose. [CCR regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/4/data.xml).

Current working classification is an ongoing bookkeeping/cloud service, based
on the existing product contract. This is an inference, not a ruling about every
supplied feature. Do not apply a downloadable-content waiver to the entire
subscription just because the app exports files. Digital-content early supply
has separate consent and acknowledgement conditions. Where classification is
genuinely disputed, escalate the specific case before a deduction or denial.
[CCR regulation 37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/37/data.xml).

For an applicable distance service contract, the ordinary cancellation period
ends 14 days after the day of contract formation. Missing cancellation
information can extend it: it is not safe to reject every older request.
[Regulation 30](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/30/data.xml),
[regulation 31](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/31/data.xml).

Starting a service early requires an express request. Mere Checkout completion
or use is not that evidence. A lawful cancellation deduction must reflect the
service actually supplied against the full contract, using the agreed price
(or market value if excessive); the required request and prior information must
exist. If those conditions were missing, do not deduct for supply during the
cancellation period. Using a continuing subscription does not mean the entire
contract has already been fully performed. [Regulation 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/36/data.xml).

An applicable service-cancellation reimbursement is due without undue delay,
within 14 days after notification, normally by the same payment means and without
a refund fee. This deadline is distinct from the issuer's time to display funds;
provider processing is not permission to extend the legal deadline.
[Regulation 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/34/data.xml).

Service quality rights are separate from change-of-mind cancellation. Reasonable
care and skill, repeat performance where available, and an appropriate price
reduction where the statutory conditions apply cannot be replaced by a Founder
discretionary policy. A section 56 refund may be the full price and has its own
14-day trigger: the trader's agreement that the consumer is entitled to it.
[CRA section 49](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/49/data.xml),
[section 55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/55/data.xml),
[section 56](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/56/data.xml).

The April 2026 official response anticipates the new subscription regime in
spring 2027, subject to legislation. This check did not establish a later
commencement. Do not present the proposed renewal cooling-off right as already
in force today; recheck commencement before release.
[Government response](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/consultation-on-the-implementation-of-the-new-subscription-contracts-regime/outcome/government-response-to-consultation-on-the-implementation-of-the-new-subscription-contracts-regime-web-accessible-version).

## Information and delivery conditions

Before a consumer is bound, provide clear characteristics, supplier identity and
required contact/address information, full price/period, performance, duration,
termination and cancellation information, including the model form where
applicable. The current PO Box is described only as correspondence: this check
has not established that it satisfies the geographical establishment-address
requirement. Do not silently label it an establishment address.
[Regulation 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/13/data.xml),
[Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/schedule/2/data.xml).

Preserve a personally addressed, unchanged contract confirmation with its terms
and consent version; a mutable web link alone is insufficient. Provide it before
service performance begins. Cancellation can be any clear statement, not only
our form; acknowledge online submissions without delay on a durable medium.
[Regulation 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/16/data.xml),
[regulation 32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/32/data.xml).

The official guidance permits a durable part of a personal account if personal
information remains unchanged and accessible for a reasonable time. A downloaded
record is useful evidence, but merely offering a download does not prove every
required document was provided. No customer-email delivery is enabled here.
[Government implementing guidance, section G](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/429300/bis-13-1368-consumer-contracts-information-cancellation-and-additional-payments-regulations-guidance.pdf).

## Founder policy — not additional law

- Upgrade Plus to Pro: quote the actual difference and credit for paid unused
  time, effective time and next charge date. Confirm first. Apply only after the
  difference is paid. No credit for unpaid or already-credited time.
- Downgrade Pro to Plus: retain the current paid period, then renew on Plus using
  the same subscription. No overlapping new subscription. Preserve other grants.
- Failed renewal: no additional free grace. Preserve unexpired funded access;
  end only the unpaid expired tier. Retention rules do not change.
- Statutory and contractual duties first. Extra goodwill: Founder decides each
  full/partial/declined case on evidence. No automatic extra window or promise.
- Full successful refund removes the corresponding funded entitlement, not
  unrelated grants or later payments. Partial refund does not automatically
  change access. Renewal cancellation is a separately recorded action.

## CS ordinary workflow

1. Receive the payment reference, reason, note and server receipt time. Preserve
   the original request. Acknowledge receipt, not approval. Do not ask for PAN,
   passwords or secrets. Accept clear cancellation notices received through
   support even if the customer does not use the form.
2. Find the payment, invoice, subscription, purchased period, succeeded/pending
   refunds, contract version and actual consumer facts. Establish legal and
   contractual obligations first. Request only missing relevant information.
3. Record basis, supporting reference, calculation, public explanation, access
   and retention consequences, and separate renewal decision. For goodwill,
   record the specific Founder approval; general policy approval is insufficient.
4. Authorised reviewer chooses full / partial / declined / needs information.
   Review alone sends no provider money request. The authorised operator submits
   the approved amount only after release activation and case authority exist.
5. Preserve operation identity and provider response. Pending, requires action,
   succeeded, failed and canceled remain distinct. An uncertain operation needs
   reconciliation; do not start a second refund with a new key.
6. On confirmation, reconcile all funded periods and independent grants. Keep
   billing case records separate from bookkeeping backups/retention deletion.
   Review case retention as personal data: this draft does not invent a fixed
   retention period or authorise a purge.

{paid-end/retention facts}. Contact support if the funds do not appear.”
