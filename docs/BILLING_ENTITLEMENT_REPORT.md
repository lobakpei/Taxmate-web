# Billing and Entitlement Report

> **SUPERSEDED HISTORICAL EVIDENCE — NOT CURRENT CONTRACT TRUTH.** This report records the earlier release state and its then-valid £7.99/month / £59.99/year Pro evidence. Founder superseded that commercial contract on 29 August 2026 with launch £9.99/month, standard £11.99/month and £99.99/year. The historical evidence below is preserved unchanged and must not be used by a current pricing gate.

Free remains permanent. Plus and Pro access resolves only from `users/{uid}/entitlements/current`, a client-read/server-write document. A local `tier`, `pro` or legacy trial flag cannot unlock paid features.

Founder-approved pricing is Free £0; Plus £3.99/month or £29.99/year; and Pro £7.99/month or £59.99/year. Monthly and yearly are recurring subscriptions. The independent TaxMate Stripe account contains matching TEST and LIVE Price objects with strict mode separation. Stripe Tax is off and TaxMate does not add VAT at checkout.

Checkout is server-priced, requires Terms acceptance outside the local emulator, rejects a second non-ended subscription and uses Billing Portal for management. A permanent Founder Pro grant blocks every paid Checkout target. Ordinary cancellation preserves paid entitlement until the selected monthly or yearly period end; full refund ends the refunded paid entitlement immediately; partial refund enters manual review. Existing monthly subscriptions are recognised as monthly and are never auto-migrated. These access transitions never delete bookkeeping data.

Founder free-access promo codes are separate from Stripe discounts. The callable reads canonical `founderPromotions/{code}` configuration in Firestore and transactionally checks start time, active state, Plus/Pro tier, fixed or permanent expiry model, global redemption limit and duplicate UID redemption. `promotionRedemptions` preserves UID, code, granted tier, redemption time, campaign start, entitlement expiry and `founder_promo` source. Paid monthly, paid annual, fixed Founder and permanent Founder grants are resolved together: any active Pro wins, otherwise any active Plus wins, otherwise Free. Promo expiry never creates or charges a Stripe subscription.

Normal clients cannot read or write Founder promo definitions or redemption truth. The non-public admin command is protected by the authenticated Firebase CLI identity and Google Cloud IAM, and supports list, view, create, atomic pending-placeholder migration, disable and specific-UID revoke without an app release. Operational instructions are in `docs/FOUNDER_PROMO_OPERATIONS.md`.

The production webhook is active and its signing secret remains stored in `taxmate-uk-2` Secret Manager. The approved restricted LIVE key was rotated without permission expansion and its replacement is stored only as enabled `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` in the same production project. Repository and frontend findings are zero. No changed production Functions were deployed, so paid Checkout remains fail-closed until an approved deploy and runtime secret-binding smoke test; it never falls back to TEST credentials or client unlock.
