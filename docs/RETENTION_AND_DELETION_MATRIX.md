# R3 receipt isolation and recovery supplement

R3 supersedes the R2 global receipt write fence: unrelated accounts and receipt objects remain writable during another object's cleanup or retry. Exact server-owned admissions reserve nested/URL/LTD receipt references; original ledger Rules still authorise the atomic mutation. Physical deletion follows a durable job and generation-specific intent, never a queued/rejected ledger write. An expired worker resumes only that intent; it cannot unlock an uncertain deletion.

Receipt retry events and a paged recovery-sweep source definition are included, but no production scheduler or deployment is activated. Account retention purge remains demo-only. A failed receipt cleanup leaves durable work and `receipt_cleanup_pending`/pending counts rather than permanently holding the account's completed record-retention epoch. Pending physical cleanup must not be shown as completed deletion. Founder and independent acceptance remain PENDING; Fable UI has not started.
# R4 admission lifecycle supplement

Ordinary/meta/LTD/shared pending admissions contain actual draft data and are covered by expiry, live-authority read restrictions, physical retention/reset cleanup and unattended recovery. Matching pins are removed transactionally or by orphan recovery; this never deletes image bytes. Plus historical LTD reads and Free ordinary operations are unchanged. Admission cleanup failure blocks retention completion with an explicit failed stage. See `R4_ADMISSION_LIFECYCLE.md` and the fresh R4 evidence; R3/R2 supplements below are historical where superseded. No production scheduler or purge activation is authorised.
