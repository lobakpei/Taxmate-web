Key conclusion: Firestore’s London region does not make the whole service UK-only. Receipt storage, support-email routing and several processors involve US/global processing. Production requires current DPAs/terms and a documented UK international-transfer assessment; provider marketing statements alone are not treated as legal approval. Support infrastructure evidence: [Namecheap email-forwarding documentation](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/308/2214/how-to-set-up-free-email-forwarding/), [Namecheap privacy policy](https://www.namecheap.com/legal/general/privacy-policy/) and [Microsoft’s current privacy statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-GB/privacy/privacystatement). The private Outlook destination address must remain confidential.
