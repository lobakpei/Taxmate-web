Using TaxMate
You must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract. By using TaxMate you agree to these terms. TaxMate helps UK self-employed users and directors keep bookkeeping records, estimate selected Self Assessment and Corporation Tax amounts and create summaries or working-paper exports. TaxMate does not file statutory accounts, CT600 returns or Companies House accounts. You are responsible for lawful and accurate input, protecting your account and devices, keeping suitable backups, reviewing calculations and meeting deadlines. Do not upload unlawful content, malware or information you lack authority to use.
Free, Plus & Pro
Free costs £0 and provides the core functions shown in the Free plan. Plus costs £3.99 per month or £29.99 per year and adds multiple businesses, receipt photos, mileage comparison and the PDF tax report. Pro includes the established Pro tools and one active Limited Company. Limited Company creation, editing, posting, calculation, restore and outbound cloud writes require effective Pro access. The approved Pro prices are the launch price of £9.99 per month, the standard monthly price of £11.99 per month and £99.99 per year. Paid or Founder-promotion Pro resolves to the same effective Limited Company access.
Prices and renewal
Before payment, Checkout shows the selected final price, renewal interval, included plan and payment method. Stripe Tax is off and no VAT amount is added to the listed price. Paid subscriptions renew until cancelled. A future price change applies only as disclosed for a future billing period. TaxMate does not describe the standard Pro price as a previous or reduced price.
Cancellation and refunds
Cancel through Manage subscription or contact support. Cancellation stops future renewal and access normally continues until the end of the paid period. Cancelling does not delete records. Consumers may have a statutory cancellation period, normally 14 days for a distance service contract. By completing Checkout and using paid features immediately, you request that the service begin during that period. Contact support to exercise a cancellation right or request the model cancellation form. A successful full refund ends the refunded paid entitlement; an independent active promotion can still apply. A partial refund is marked for manual review and does not automatically change access. Nothing removes mandatory statutory rights.
Promotion codes
A valid TaxMate promotion grants fixed-period or permanent Plus or Pro access. It requires no card, creates no Stripe subscription and never starts an automatic charge by itself. A fixed campaign ends at its stated expiry even if redeemed later. Disabling a code stops future redemptions but does not cancel grants already redeemed. A specific revocation is separate. TaxMate applies the highest active paid or promotional tier, and records remain preserved when access changes.
Tax estimates, HMRC and Companies House
TaxMate provides planning estimates and working aids, not tax, accounting or legal advice. It does not file Self Assessment returns, MTD updates, statutory accounts, CT600 Corporation Tax returns or Companies House accounts, is not HMRC submission software and is not affiliated with HMRC or Companies House. Exports are not official filings or proof that an authority will accept a treatment. Verify important figures with current guidance or a qualified adviser.
Accounts and partnerships
Google is the only sign-in provider. A partnership code grants access to shared records, so share it only with an intended partner. Members can see shared records; new collaboration and write actions require Pro. If Pro ends, authorised historical access remains but new Pro-only collaboration stops. Account deletion removes your membership; records remain for other members and are deleted when the last member leaves.
Data and backups
You retain rights in your records and permit TaxMate to process them only to provide the service as you direct. Keep suitable backups. A former Pro user can read and export existing Limited Company data but cannot create, edit, post, calculate, restore or send new Limited Company cloud writes without Pro. Use the separate deletion control if you want account data removed.
