TaxMate UK help

TaxMate helps UK sole traders and self-employed people keep income, expense, mileage and receipt records together and see an estimated tax bill as they go.

What TaxMate does

Add your income and expenses, organise records by tax year and review an estimated tax position. Estimates depend on the information you enter and should be checked before you rely on them.

Tax returns and Making Tax Digital

TaxMate helps you keep records and understand your figures. It does not submit your tax return or MTD updates to HMRC. If MTD applies, use software that HMRC lists for submission and verify current requirements directly with HMRC.

Backups

Use Back up data in Settings. A JSON export contains app records but no receipt-image binaries. A full ZIP backup includes available receipt binaries and a manifest. Keep a separate copy somewhere you control.

Account deletion

For a signed-in account, use Delete all my data. Deletion is complete only when the app reports server-side success; otherwise contact support so cloud deletion can be retried.
