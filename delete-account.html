Delete your TaxMate account and data

Choose the in-app control or request deletion if you cannot access the app.

Delete in the app

Sign in to the TaxMate account you want to delete. Open Settings → Delete my TaxMate account and data, review the warning and confirm. Export any records you need first. Deletion is complete only when TaxMate confirms server-side success.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot use the app, email support@taxmate.uk with the subject TaxMate account deletion and the email address used to sign in. We may ask you to verify ownership securely before deleting the account. Do not send your password, payment-card details or receipt images.

Request account deletion

What is deleted and what may remain?

TaxMate deletes your TaxMate sign-in identity, personal cloud records and receipt files after required billing checks. This does not delete your Google or Apple account. Shared partnership records remain for other members; your membership is removed. Limited deletion, billing and security records may be retained where required for legal obligations, refunds, disputes or fraud prevention. These records cannot restore your deleted TaxMate account. See the Privacy policy for details.

If a subscription is active, pending or uncertain, cancellation or reconciliation with Google Play, the App Store or Stripe may be needed first. Email us if you cannot complete that step.
